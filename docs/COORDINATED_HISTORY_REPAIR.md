# Closed coordinated history conditions

The exposed development query `培训主题：窑炉巡检\n培训对象：车间班组长\n必须有实际授课记录，并且必须以前给这类学员讲完这门课` incorrectly left its second clause unresolved even when the source contained a complete personal event for that exact course and audience. The old failure is preserved in the private `yanxu-compound-history-repair.EXiubEy3/before.log`; the earlier complete probe remains unchanged.

`RequirementNarrative` now permits a single positive coordination prefix (`并且 / 并 / 且 / 同时`) and explicit mandatory modal words in its existing closed past-teaching predicate. The entire clause, course reference and audience reference keep their original spans. A unique course and a unique audience are still required for their respective references. There is no trimming of a condition, negation, alternative or unknown tail.

`CoordinatedAudienceHistoryTest` has 184 assertions over two unrelated topics, four conjunctions, three modal forms, exact event support, wrong audience, other teacher, future plan, cross-course borrowing, complete provenance, unknown qualification, ambiguous course/audience references and post-object syntax. The new test fails against the previous frozen classes and passes with the parser overlay. This is a source-grammar regression repair, not model post-training, a fresh blind evaluation, or proof that all compound natural language is supported.

Ten existing narrative/topic/audience/contract suites also pass with the overlay. An actual no-model `PosttrainingV2Probe` prepare→coverage→selection replay used five candidates: correct own completed event, wrong audience, other teacher, future plan and cross-course audience borrowing. The prior frozen classes admit none; the corrected overlay admits only the supported candidate.
